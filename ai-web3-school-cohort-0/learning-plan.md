# Personal Learning Plan

## Principle

Use the Handbook as the knowledge map and WCB as the official learning/check-in platform. The repo is the public proof-of-work workspace: notes, tasks, experiments, feedback, and project records.

## Starting Path

Because the learner profile is not confirmed yet, start with a balanced foundation path:

1. AI foundations: LLM, Prompt, Context, RAG, Agent.
2. Web3 foundations: Network, Cryptography, Wallet, Smart Contract, Account Abstraction.
3. Bridge topics: Chain-aware Context, Web3 Tool Use, Agent Workflow, Agent Wallet.
4. Project direction: choose one small prototype from Dev Tooling, AI Security/Privacy, Agentic Commerce/Payment, or Governance/Coordination.

## Daily Rhythm

### Minimal Path, 30-45 minutes

- Read one Handbook section.
- Write 3-5 bullet notes in `daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- Record one question or feedback item.
- Prepare a short WCB check-in draft.

### Recommended Path, 60-90 minutes

- Read one Handbook section and one linked or related topic.
- Convert the concept into a small task note.
- Run or design one tiny experiment.
- Submit/check in manually on WCB or the required platform.

### Challenge Path, 2+ hours

- Build a small proof-of-work artifact.
- Connect AI and Web3 concepts in one design note or demo.
- Prepare one Handbook feedback item with a concrete suggested change.

## Week 1 Focus

- Establish shared vocabulary across AI and Web3.
- Learn the boundaries of agents, context, tool use, wallets, signing, and permissions.
- Produce at least five daily notes and one feedback item.

## Weekly Review

Every week, summarize:

- What I learned.
- What I built or tested.
- What confused me.
- Which Handbook page could be improved.
- What I will do next week.
